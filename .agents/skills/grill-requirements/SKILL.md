# Clarify the request

1. Restate the requested outcome in simple words.
2. Ask one important question at a time. Prefer questions that change the work.
3. Check these areas when they apply:
   - Who uses it and what they need.
   - Current behavior and desired behavior.
   - What is out of scope.
   - Failure cases and unusual inputs.
   - Data, privacy, permissions, and migration needs.
   - How the user will know the work succeeded.
4. Research questions that the repository can answer instead of asking the user.
5. End with a short agreed brief: goal, boundaries, decisions, unanswered questions, and how success will be checked.

Do not fill gaps with guesses. Do not write the implementation plan until important questions are answered, deliberately deferred, or accepted as risks.
