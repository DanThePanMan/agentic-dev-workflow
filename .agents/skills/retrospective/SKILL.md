# Learn from the work

1. Compare what was wanted with what was delivered.
2. Note proof that the result works, friction, rework, surprises, and remaining risks.
3. Suggest at most three improvements to project notes, templates, skills, tools, or working habits.
4. Explain what evidence supports each suggestion and how much it may help.
5. Save suggestions as candidates for approval. Do not silently change skills, project rules, tools, or settings.

Keep this short. Prefer a useful next improvement over a long meeting summary.
