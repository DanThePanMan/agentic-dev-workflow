# Save the useful lesson

1. Confirm that the work is substantial and safe to record. Remove secrets and sensitive details.
2. Create a clearly named file, for example `case-studies/login-migration.md`.
3. Explain the starting situation, limits, important decisions, approach, result, and proof.
4. Link the related specification, plan, changed code, checks, and retrospective when they exist.
5. End with lessons that say when they apply and when they may not.
6. Ask before saving or sharing details that may be sensitive.

Write a useful example, not a diary of every command.
